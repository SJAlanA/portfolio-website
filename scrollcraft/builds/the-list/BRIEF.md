## The feeling curve

One line per act: the emotion, then what on screen causes it.

```
1  Clarity        Title page. His name at poster scale, the role, "from mid-2027",
                  Cornell, and a five-line list. Nothing is ambiguous.
2  Calm           Chapter One. Cornell in a quiet spread: one photograph in its own
                  column, measured prose. Nothing moves except what the hand moves.
3  Anticipation   An authored silence. A black viewport and two small words,
                  "Chapter Two". The visitor slows down.
4  Impact (PEAK)  The cut. A hairline draws across the whole screen on a diagonal, the
                  black card splits along it and the two halves slide apart, and
                  yellow floods in behind with the security work.
5  Respect        Chapter Two spread. Real figures land in the prose:
                  4 hours to 30 minutes, 17 engineer-hours per drop, 2 design changes.
6  Warmth         Chapter Three. People: 150+ students, 14+ projects, a festival of
                  1,000+. The scale shifts from machines to humans.
7  Curiosity      Chapter Four. An index of projects, one schema per entry, read like
                  museum labels.
8  Resolve        The colophon. The last item is crossed off, the list reads complete,
                  and the line under it is the way to reach him. It holds.
```

No two adjacent acts share a feeling.

## The peak

It lives in **act 4, the cut**, at the start of Chapter Two. It gets the largest
scroll span on the page, the silence in front of it (act 3), and the best
generated frame (the ECU test bench).
